# Digital Growth image assets

This directory contains the public-facing client logos, covers, and social creatives used by the Digital Growth case studies.

## Folder map

- `euphoric/`: logo, Facebook cover and post, Instagram post
- `home-cure/`: logo, Facebook cover and posts
- `root-cyber/`: logo, Facebook cover and posts
- `clear-voice-hub/`: logo, Facebook cover and posts
- `ama-auditing/`: logo, Facebook cover and posts

The files were captured from the public Facebook and Instagram profile URLs recorded in `src/data/brandSystems.ts`. They are stored locally so the portfolio does not depend on third-party hotlinks or login state.

Keep every asset mapped through its project `gallery` entry with descriptive alt text, platform, format, aspect ratio, caption, and `available: true`. Only publish creative the portfolio owner is authorized to showcase. Campaign performance data is intentionally not inferred from these images.
